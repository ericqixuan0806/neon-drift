Neon Drift arcade game. Collect cyan orbs and dodge pink hazards. Move with left and right arrow keys or A and D. In two-player co-op, Player 1 uses A and D and Player 2 uses the arrow keys; score and lives are shared. Press Enter or Space to start or select the focused game canvas action. Press P or Escape to pause or resume, Q to quit while paused, and M to toggle sound. During play, use keys 1 through 4 for unlocked skills. In solo play, touch and mouse players can drag to steer; both co-op pilots use their keyboard controls. Press Tab to reach the accessible game actions and settings.
