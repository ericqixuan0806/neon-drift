Neon Drift arcade game. Collect cyan orbs and dodge pink hazards. Move with left and right arrow keys or A and D. Press Enter or Space to start or select the focused game canvas action. Press P or Escape to pause or resume, Q to quit while paused, and M to toggle sound. During play, use keys 1 through 4 for unlocked skills. Touch and mouse players can drag to steer and tap the canvas menu controls. Press Tab to reach the accessible game actions and settings.
